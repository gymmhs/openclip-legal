OpenClip Studio

OpenClip Studio is a desktop tool for creators. It helps a creator cut short vertical clips from videos they have the rights to use, review each clip, write the caption, and publish it to their own TikTok accounts after confirming who can see it and what viewers may do with it.

How it works with TikTok

  • The creator logs in with TikTok Login Kit and chooses which of their own accounts to use.
  • For each clip, the app shows the account's name and avatar, a preview of the video, an editable caption, a required choice of who can view the post, switches for comments / Duet / Stitch (off by default), a commercial-content disclosure, and TikTok's required agreement.
  • Nothing is posted until the creator confirms in the app. The video is sent with the Content Posting API.
