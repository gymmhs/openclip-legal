Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what OpenClip Studio (the "App", operated by fullini) does with information when you connect your TikTok account.

What we receive from TikTok

  • When you log in with TikTok Login Kit: an access token and refresh token for your account, the permissions you granted, and your account's open ID.
  • From TikTok's creator-info service: your display name, username, avatar, the visibility options your account allows and its posting limits. They are shown to you so you can see which account a clip will go to.
  • The status of videos you publish (for example "processing" or "published").

What we do with it

We use this information only to show you the account you are posting to and to publish the clips you confirm. We do not sell it, rent it, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

Where it is stored

The App runs on your own computer. Tokens are stored in your computer's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain). Your clips, captions and settings stay on your computer. We do not operate a server that receives your TikTok data.

Sharing

The video you publish and its caption are sent to TikTok through its official API. We do not share TikTok data with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

  • Click Disconnect in the App to delete the stored login from your computer immediately.
  • You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.
  • To ask us to delete anything we hold about you, write to fullini69@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by changing this page. Questions: fullini69@gmail.com.
